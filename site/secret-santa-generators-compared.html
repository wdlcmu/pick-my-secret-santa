Free Secret Santa generators, tested

Seven free generators, checked the same way, ours included. The short version: four of them ask nobody for an email or an account. They differ in who can see the matches, and that difference matters more than the feature lists.

How we checked

On November 20, 2026 we read each tool's own pages and help docs, field by field: what you hand over to start, who can see the matches, whether couples can be kept apart, the size limit, late joiners, wish lists, and anonymous questions. Where a claim comes from a tool's own pages rather than a finished draw, this page says so. We also cross-checked one published hands-on test: Giftruly's October 14, 2026 test, which ran the same five-person draw, with a couple kept apart, on 11 tools and counted the clicks. The click counts below are theirs. Our own tool's time is our measured median for organizers: 73 seconds.

One tool we could not check at all: TinyToolz, which some lists still name. Its domain showed a for-sale page when we checked on November 20, 2026, so there was no working tool to test. It is left out of the table rather than guessed about.

What we found, tool by tool

Pick My Secret Santa (ours)

The fastest start of the seven: no email, no account, no app, and the draw is done in the same visit, median 73 seconds in our own measurements. Each person gets a private link, and the organizer can take part without any match showing on their screen. The honest drawbacks are in the next section, and they are real.

Giftruly

The most complete free tool we checked. One group link for the chat, or a private link per person; wishes each person writes themselves; anonymous questions; a latecomer joins without a redraw. Two catches: the organizer must leave an email to get back to the group, and the group link shows the whole roster, with each person tapping their own name on trust.

Secret Noël

The strongest secrecy of the seven, on its own description: the draw runs on their server, the organizer never sees the results, and everything is deleted 30 days after the exchange. Wish lists and anonymous messages included, groups up to 5,000, English and French. The trade: your group confirms and fills in wish lists before the draw, so it asks more of everyone up front.

Linguaholic

The in-the-room option. The draw stays on the organizer's device, matches ride inside the links in a scrambled form the site itself says is not encryption, and one phone can be passed around instead of sending links. Wish lists are typed in by the organizer from a questionnaire. The wider site carries ads; the generator page says the draw itself runs in your browser.

GiftList (free generator)

The quickest bare draw: names, exclusions, done in 8 clicks in the October test, with the results carried in the link itself and nothing stored on a server. The price is stated on its own page: anyone with the results link can see every pairing. Send each person their own link and keep the results link to yourself.

DrawNames

The deepest feature set of the seven on paper: wish lists, gift reserving, avoiding last year's matches, people joining or leaving after the draw. On the website it costs emails: the organizer's, with a sign-in code, and one for each person. In the October test it stopped at click 12, at the email step. Its app skips email entirely.

Elfster

The heavyweight for big office exchanges, with wish lists and questions, and the heaviest start: an account for every person who wants to see their own name. For a group that will not sign up for anything, it is out before it starts.

Our own drawbacks, plainly. The organizer holds every person's link on the manage page, and could open one: the links are hidden from plain sight, not locked, and the names and matches are stored on our server in a form we can read. There are no wish lists and no anonymous questions. A draw is final once the links are made, so a late joiner means drawing again or swapping in a volunteer. One draw holds at most 100 people. If any of those is the thing your group needs, Giftruly or Secret Noël will suit you better, and this page just told you so.

Which one fits your group

Nobody should hand over an email, organizer included: Pick My Secret Santa, Linguaholic, Secret Noël, or the free GiftList generator.

One link in the group chat, and the group taps its own names: Giftruly. That is its home ground. Know that the roster is visible to anyone with the link.

The organizer must have no way to see the matches at all: Secret Noël, drawn on their server, or Giftruly, where the organizer never holds a link. Ours does not meet this bar, and we say so above.

Wish lists matter more than speed: Giftruly or Secret Noël, where each person writes their own, or DrawNames if your group is fine with email.

A very large group: Secret Noël, up to 5,000. Giftruly and Linguaholic reach 200.

Everyone is in one room: Linguaholic's pass-the-phone, or Secret Noël's hat mode and printable cards.

Your group speaks Spanish or Portuguese: Pick My Secret Santa runs the full draw in both. Giftruly covers Spanish among its five languages.

Common questions

Which free Secret Santa generator asks for nothing at all?

Pick My Secret Santa, Linguaholic, Secret Noël and the free GiftList generator all start with no account and no email from anyone, organizer included. Giftruly asks the organizer for an email after the group is created. DrawNames asks the organizer for an email and a sign-in code on its website.

Which generator keeps the organizer from seeing the matches?

Secret Noël draws on its server and says the organizer never sees the results. Giftruly never hands the organizer anyone's link. On Pick My Secret Santa the organizer's screen shows names and links only, but the organizer holds every link and could open one: hidden, not locked. On the free GiftList generator, anyone with the results link can see every pairing, and the page says so.

What if someone joins after the draw?

Giftruly adds a latecomer into its circle and only one or two people get a new name. DrawNames says people can be added or removed after the draw. On Pick My Secret Santa the draw is final once the links are made: draw the whole group again, or swap in a volunteer.

Which generator is best for a very large group?

Secret Noël takes 3 to 5,000 people. Giftruly and Linguaholic take up to 200. Pick My Secret Santa and GiftList's full exchange take up to 100.
