What goes on the gift

The rules this site prints are short: their name on the gift. Secret until the swap. At the swap, each person opens, then guesses out loud before the giver owns up — that is when your name comes out, in person, not on the tag.

Mailing it? Keep the box plain too

Mail it in a plain, sturdy box. Write no return name or address. A name on the box gives the game away before the party starts.

Pick the reveal before the day

Groups here pick one reveal game before the day: Guess first, Three clues, or Balloon pop. The reveal comes last — after every gift is open.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

What are the basic Secret Santa rules?

At least three people. One budget everyone can afford. Each person draws one name, never their own. Buy one gift inside the budget. Label it with their name only, and stay secret until the swap.

Do we have to reveal at the end?

No. It is your group's call. Some groups never reveal. Most do, because the guessing is the fun part.
