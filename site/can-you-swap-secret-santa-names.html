Can you swap Secret Santa names?

Not one match by itself. A finished draw has no way to edit or swap just one name. There are two honest ways, and only two — the same two this site gives for adding someone late or fixing a drop-out.

Way one: draw the whole group again

Every secret kept. Draw the whole group again, and every match is drawn fresh. Send only the new set of links — old links still show the old draw. Tell the group only that the draw ran again. Nobody has bought a gift yet? This is the way. It costs nothing but new links.

Way two: a volunteer swaps

One person gives up their match, and takes the other match instead. Three matches change, and the group hears nothing. The price, named out loud: the volunteer tells the organizer their match, and the organizer learns those matches too. One corner of the seal is spent. Gifts are bought and links are out? This is the way.

Someone dropped out instead?

Same pair of fixes. Draw again with the name taken out, or a volunteer gives to the person who lost their Santa. Both fixes get harder once gifts are bought — say so the day you know.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Can I just swap one match?

No. A finished draw has no way to swap or edit one match. Pick one of the two ways on this page instead.

Does the volunteer way break the secret?

Partly. The volunteer tells the organiser their match, and the organiser learns those matches too. The rest of the group hears nothing.
