    Give it without giving yourself away

    Pick the way that fits your place.

    Desk drop. Leave it on their desk before work starts. Slip away before they sit down. Best when you know their start time.

    Doorstep drop. Set the gift at their front door. Knock once, then walk away fast. Rain coming? Put it under cover first.

    Front desk hold. Leave it with the front desk or mail room. Ask staff to hand it over later. Put only their name on the label.

    Helper handoff. Ask a friend outside the game to deliver it. They say one line only: this is for you. Pick someone who will not spill the name.

    Locker stash. Slip it into their locker, cubby, or drawer. Use a spot they open every day. Make sure no one else can reach it.

    Mailbox surprise. Drop it in their work mailbox slot. Add a tag that says: open later, alone. Small, soft gifts fit this way best.

    Plain mail box. Mail it in a plain, sturdy box. Write no return name or address. Post it early. Holiday mail runs slow.

    Clue trail. Hide the gift somewhere safe nearby. Leave a first clue where they will see it. Each clue leads to the next one. The last clue leads to the gift.

    Decoy box. Put the gift inside a bigger, silly box. Wrap that box in plain paper. The odd shape hides what is inside. Add soft filler so nothing rattles.

    Gift table drop. Put it on the shared gift table early. Arrive before your person does. Then join the crowd like everyone else.

    For every way: use plain wrap. Print the tag. Handwriting gives you away.

    When you swap gifts

    Pick one game for the whole group.

    Guess first. Open one gift at a time, in turns. Each person guesses their Santa first. Only then does the Santa speak up.

    Tag clue round. Write one clue on the gift tag. Read the tag aloud before opening. The group guesses from the clue alone.

    Three clues game. Each Santa gives three clues, one by one. Clues start hard and get easier. The person guesses after each clue.

    Note inside the wrap. Hide a signed note inside the wrapping paper. Your person finds it while they open. Keep the outside of the gift blank.

    Stand and point. After the last gift, all Santas stand up. Each person points at their best guess. Santas sit down when guessed right.

    Balloon pop. Put each Santa name inside a balloon. Each person pops one after opening their gift. The name inside names their Santa.

    Video reveal. Film a short clip before the party. Start in a hat or a mask. Take it off and say your name. Play the clips after the last gift.

    All at once. Tell everyone at once after the last gift. No guessing, no games, all names together. A Santa can stay secret if they want.
