Common questions

Where do these travel ideas come from?

They come from a fixed list of 212 real ideas. Every card on this page is one of the 21 ideas the bank tags travel.

How many travel ideas does each person get?

Six ideas ride in each private link, inside the group budget. Picks tagged travel are ranked first inside their budget band.

What if my budget band has few travel ideas?

The $50-$100 band holds four travel ideas. A link in that band fills the rest of its six with other ideas from the same band.

What about a young traveler?

Two cards on this page are picked for young travelers. They sit in their own section, labeled for young travelers.

Are the budget bands exact prices?

No. Budget bands are guides, not exact prices. The site links to no stores, sells nothing, and takes no payment.
