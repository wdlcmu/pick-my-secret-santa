Yankee swap rules

Yankee Swap is a swap with steals. Each guest brings one wrapped gift. The group sets one price cap first. You open gifts in turn. A turn can end with a steal.

The rules, step by step

  1. Bring one wrapped gift near the budget.
  2. Write one number per slip. All draw one slip.
  3. Number one picks a wrapped gift. They open it for all to see.
  4. The next guest makes a choice. Open a new gift, or steal an opened one.
  5. If your gift gets stolen, pick once more. Open a fresh one, or steal one back.
  6. Most groups cap steals on one gift. Three steals in a round is the norm.
  7. Play ends when every gift stands open.
  8. Number one takes the last move. They may swap for any opened gift.

House rules bend these steps. Set the steal cap first. That one call stops most fights.

Some groups play a variant. Some let a player whose gift was stolen wait and pick again at the end. Some skip the first player's final swap. Name your group's variant before the first gift opens.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. It runs on trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Is Yankee Swap the same as Dirty Santa and White Elephant?

Nearly. Yankee Swap, Dirty Santa, and White Elephant are close cousins. The name shifts from place to place. The steal game stays the same.

How is Yankee Swap not Secret Santa?

Secret Santa gives each guest one name in secret. This game fights for gifts in the open. No names get drawn at all.
