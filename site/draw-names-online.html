How the draw works

You draw names online in five steps.

  1. Name your group. Pick a gift budget.
  2. Add every name. Add what each person likes.
  3. Mark pairs to keep apart. Only if some must not draw each other.
  4. Tap Draw the names. The draw runs in your browser.
  5. Send each private link. Send it to its person only.

No one draws their own name.

You need at least three people.
