Common questions

Is foodie an interest in the draw?

The draw calls it cooking. Set cooking on a person's row, and their link carries picks tagged cooking first.

Where do these food ideas come from?

They come from a fixed bank of 212 real ideas. Every card on this page is one of the 26 ideas the bank tags cooking.

How many ideas does each person get?

Six. Each private link carries six gift ideas inside the group budget, picked for the interest on that person's row.

What if my budget band has few cooking ideas?

The $100+ band holds five cooking ideas. A link in that band fills the last spot with another idea from the same band.

Is one card on this page for a child?

Yes. The cookie kit is sized for a child baking with a grown-up. Its card says so in plain words.

Are the budget bands exact prices?

No. Budget bands are guides, not exact prices. The site links to no stores, sells nothing, and takes no payment.
