Common questions

Do students need accounts or emails?

No. Nobody in the class makes an account or gives an email. The teacher adds the names and sends or shows each private link.

How many students can be in one draw?

One draw holds 3 to 30 people, which covers most classes. A bigger class splits into smaller draws, one for each table or group.

Can the teacher see who drew whom?

Not on the screen. The organizer screen shows names and links, never matches. The teacher holds every link and could open one — a link is hidden from plain sight, not locked.

What if I close the tab before sending a link?

Your links stay saved on this device. Open this page again to send one. Nothing is saved on our server.
