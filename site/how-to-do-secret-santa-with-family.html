How to do Secret Santa with family

One draw for the whole family. Each person buys one gift, for one person, inside one budget the whole family agrees on. Names stay secret until the swap.

The family run, step by step

Pick the gift exchange day. Agree one gift budget — most groups land between $10 and $30, and one number for everyone keeps it fair. Collect every name, and what each person likes. Add the names here. Mark pairs to keep apart — partners, say, or the same household. Tap Draw the names. Send each private link to its person only. On the day, end with a guessing game.

Kids can play too

The draw's bank holds 60 kid picks, so a child in the draw still gets ideas that fit. Set a buy-by date — a week before the exchange works for most groups — so nobody is shopping on the day.

Family far away?

Distance does not end the game. Run the same draw online and swap by mail or on a call — see Virtual Secret Santa.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Can kids play with the grown-ups?

Yes. Kids go in the same draw. The bank's kid picks mean their link still deals six ideas inside the budget.

What budget is fair for a family?

Any one number the whole group can afford. Most groups land between $10 and $30. Say the number before the draw.
