What if someone drops out of Secret Santa?

It is fixable — if gifts are not bought yet. The clean fixes are the same two as adding someone late: draw the whole group again, or swap in a volunteer.

Start your draw

Way one: draw the whole group again

Take the name out and run the draw again. Everyone gets a new link. A new draw makes new links — and links from the old draw still work, so send the new ones and say plainly that the old ones are dead.

Way two: a volunteer swaps

One person agrees to give to the person who lost their Santa. Nobody else's match changes. This works best in small groups, where a full redraw feels like a big deal.

Tell the organizer early

Both fixes get harder once gifts are bought. If you must drop out, say so the day you know. Your own link just goes unused — it reveals nothing about anyone else.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

We already drew. Now what?

Draw again, or swap in a volunteer. It is the same pair of fixes as adding someone after the draw.

What if their Santa already bought a gift?

Then it is the group's call. The gift can still be given at the swap. Most groups would rather get the gift than follow the rule.
