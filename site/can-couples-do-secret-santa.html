Can couples do Secret Santa?

Yes. Couples play like everyone else — and the draw can keep them apart. Mark the two names as one pair before the draw. Neither of them can draw the other. Every pair you mark is honored.

Why groups keep couples apart

Two people who already buy for each other get no surprise from drawing each other. People who share a home have the same problem — a surprise is hard to hide under one roof. The exclusions page lists both as the pairs groups keep apart most, along with last year's matches.

How to mark a pair

Name your group. Pick a gift budget. Add every name. Mark each pair who must not draw each other — one pair takes two names, and you can add as many pairs as the group needs. Tap Draw the names. Send each private link to its person only.

The honest edges

Too many pairs can block the draw. With three people, one pair apart leaves no fair draw — the site says so before any link is made, in its own words: that draw cannot work. Remove one pair you marked, and the draw runs. With four people and pairs marked, one match can give away another.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Can couples or partners be kept apart?

Yes. Mark the two names as one pair before the draw. Neither of them can draw the other.

What if the pairs leave no fair draw?

If the pairs leave no fair draw, the site says so before any link is made. Remove one pair you marked, and draw again.
