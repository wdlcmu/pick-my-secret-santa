Common questions

Does this site send the invitation?

No. This site sends nothing and collects nothing. You copy the words and send them in your own chat or mail.

What budget should the invitation name?

One band for the whole group: Under $25, $25 to $50, $50 to $100, or over $100. Pick the lowest band the whole group can afford.

Does the invitation need a swap day?

Yes, name one. This site holds no date for you, so the day lives in your message, not on this site.

What happens after people reply?

Add each name, set one interest per person, mark pairs to keep apart, and draw. Each person then gets their own private link.
