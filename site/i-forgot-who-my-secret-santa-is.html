Lost the link itself?

Ask your organizer for a fresh one — only trust a link your organizer sent you. Your last draw stays saved on the organizer's device. The saved list shows one card per person, and each card names only its owner, outside its link. The organizer sends that person's link again, to its person only.

The honest limits

Only the newest draw is saved, on the same device, in the same browser. A different phone or computer has no saved draw to send from. Nothing sits on a server — the draw runs in the browser, and our server never sees your names or your matches.

Forgot who has you, instead?

That one stays secret on purpose. The organizer's screen shows names and links only, never the matches. Each match lives inside its private link only. Guessing is half the party — that answer comes at the swap, not before.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.
