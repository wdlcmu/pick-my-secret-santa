The remote run, step by step

Agree one budget and one mail-by day. Add every name, from three to thirty people. Mark pairs who must not draw each other. Draw the names in your browser. Send each person their own private link — a link opens anywhere, any town, any time zone. Mail each gift in a plain, sturdy box, early. Meet on a video call and open together.

Why it works spread out

The draw runs in your browser. No names reach a server. Gift ideas come with the match — each person gets six ideas inside the budget. Remote and in-room mix fine, so a hybrid team plays as one group.

Reveal on the call

Video reveal fits a call. Each Santa films a short clip first. Start in a hat or a mask. Take it off and say your name. Play the clips after the last gift is opened. Guess first works too.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Does the site email my team for me?

No. The site never emails or texts anyone. You send each private link yourself, in the chat or mail thread your team already uses.

How many people can join one remote draw?

One draw holds 3 to 30 people. A bigger team splits into smaller draws, one per group or floor.
