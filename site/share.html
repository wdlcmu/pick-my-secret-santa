I built a free Secret Santa draw tool with no sign-up and no emails. One organizer runs the whole draw I built this, so treat this as a maker post, not a review. Pick My Secret Santa exists because every draw tool I tried wanted an account, a pile of email addresses, or showed the organizer the matches. Here one organizer finishes the whole draw alone in about two minutes: type the names, get a private link for each person, send each link to its person. A link shows only that person's match. The organizer can see who has opened theirs, and can be in the draw without seeing anyone's match. No sign-up, no emails from anyone, no ads, free: https://pickmysecretsanta.com/ If you run a draw with it, I would genuinely like to hear what felt slow or confusing.
