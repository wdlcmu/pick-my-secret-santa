Can you do Secret Santa with an odd number of people?

Yes. Odd numbers work fine. The draw is a ring. Each person gives to one person and gets from one person. Odd or even never enters into it.

Why the ring always closes

Every name goes in once. Every name gets drawn once. No one draws their own name — the draw cannot deal it, ever. With 5, 7, or 9 people, the ring still closes. Nobody is left out, and nobody is left over.

The only real floor is three

Two people cannot play — each would know the other's match. Three works, with one honest edge: one match can give away the rest. After three, any number works, odd or even.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Does an odd number leave someone without a gift?

No. Each person draws exactly one name, and each name is drawn exactly once. The count of givers always equals the count of gifts.

Can pairs change the math?

You can mark pairs to keep apart, like partners. If the pairs leave no fair draw, the site says so before anyone gets a link.
