Common questions

Where do these book ideas come from?

They come from a fixed list of 212 real ideas. Every card on this page is one of the 22 ideas the bank tags books.

How many books ideas does each person get?

Six ideas ride in each private link, inside the group budget. Picks tagged books are ranked first inside their budget band.

What if my budget band has few books ideas?

The $100+ band holds three books ideas. A link in that band fills the rest of its six with other ideas from the same band.

What about a young reader?

Five cards on this page are picked for young readers. They sit in their own section, labeled for young readers.

Are the budget bands exact prices?

No. Budget bands are guides, not exact prices. The site links to no stores, sells nothing, and takes no payment.
