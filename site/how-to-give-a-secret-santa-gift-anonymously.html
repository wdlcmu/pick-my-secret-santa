Four ways to hand it over at work

Desk drop: leave it on their desk before work starts, and slip away before they sit down. Front desk hold: leave it with the front desk or mail room, with only their name on the label. Mailbox surprise: drop it in their work mailbox slot — small, soft gifts fit this way best. Gift table drop: put it on the shared gift table early, and arrive before your person does.

Sending it by mail instead?

Mail it in a plain, sturdy box. Write no return name or address — a name on the box gives the game away before the party starts. Post it early. Holiday mail runs slow.

Stay secret until the swap

The classic rule: secret until the swap. At the swap, each person opens, then guesses out loud before the giver owns up. That is when your name comes out — in person, not on the tag.
