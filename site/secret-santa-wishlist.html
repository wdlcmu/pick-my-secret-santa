Add a note, one person at a time

On Step 2, open the row's optional part — its label is: Add likes or a wishlist note — optional. The note field itself is labeled: Wishlist note for their Santa (optional). Type one short line. The field caps the note at 200 characters, so keep it to the thing itself.

Adding a whole list at once?

Paste a list instead: one name on each line. Add an interest after a comma if you want — Maria, cooking. A wishlist note can follow it, as a third part of the same line. The draw's own example runs: Maria, cooking, the blue scarf, size M.

Where the note shows up

When a Santa opens their match, a note saved for their person shows under the heading Their wishlist. The words under it are the site's own: Your organizer saved this note on this device. It was never sent anywhere, and it is not in your link.

The one honest limit

A note shows only when the Santa opens their link on this device — the organizer's device, the one the draw was saved on. Send the link to the Santa's own phone and the link still works: it shows their match and their six gift ideas. The note is not in it, by design, so it cannot show there. Groups who need the note seen plan for that: the Santa opens the link on the organizer's device, or the organizer reads the note out another way.

What people write

The site's own example is the whole guide: the blue scarf, size M. The thing, plus the one detail that picks the right one — a size, a color, an exact title. One line is enough; the six gift ideas in the link do the rest.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Does the wishlist note go inside the private link?

No. Wishlist notes stay on this device and never go in the links. The note was never sent anywhere, and it is not in your link.

Why can't my Santa see my wishlist note?

A note shows only when the Santa opens their link on this device — the device the organizer saved the draw on. On any other device the link still shows the match and six gift ideas, but no note rides with it.
