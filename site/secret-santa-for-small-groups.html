The honest edge of three

Three people can draw, and nobody draws their own name. The edge, in plain words: with three people, one match gives away the rest. Once you know your match, the other two fall into place. With four people and pairs marked, one match can give away another. Four or more keeps the secret stronger.

Small group tips the draw supports

How many people do you need for Secret Santa?

At least three people. Up to 30 in one draw here. Two is just a trade — each person would know exactly who has them.

Can pairs change the math?

You can mark pairs to keep apart, like partners. If the pairs leave no fair draw, the site says so before anyone gets a link.
