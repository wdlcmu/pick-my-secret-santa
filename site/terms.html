Terms

These terms cover this website and its ChatGPT plugin. The site is a free Secret Santa tool: you add names and a budget, and it draws matches and suggests gifts. There are no ads, no accounts or sign-ups, and the site sells nothing at all.

How the draw works

The draw is random, with two rules: nobody draws their own name, and pairs you mark never draw each other. The draw runs in your browser. Some rule sets cannot form a draw, and the site will say so when that happens.

The ChatGPT plugin

Our Pick My Secret Santa plugin for ChatGPT is published and live, and this part tells you how it works. The plugin sets a group up inside ChatGPT and hands back one pre-filled link to this site. It never draws or assigns anyone; the draw happens here, in your browser.

Where gift ideas come from

Gift ideas come from a fixed list: Santa's 212, drawn from the shared 255-idea bank it shares with The Present Picker. Your budget is a hard filter, and a gift hint raises matching ideas. Budget bands are guides, not exact prices; stores set real prices, and prices change.

Buying your gift

This site links to no stores. It takes no payment from you and places no orders for you. You choose where and what to buy, so check each idea before you buy it.
