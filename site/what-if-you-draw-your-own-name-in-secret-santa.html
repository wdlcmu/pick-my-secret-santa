What if you draw your own name in Secret Santa?

Here, it cannot happen. No one draws their own name. The draw cannot deal it, ever. That rule is built in, always — it is not a setting you have to turn on.

In a paper draw: draw again

The classic rules this site prints are short: each person draws one name, never their own. If it happens with names in a hat, draw again. That is the whole fix, and every group plays it that way.

What this draw does instead

The draw runs in your browser and deals one private link per person. Each link shows one match only, and a match is never the person's own name. If marked pairs leave no fair draw, the site says so before any link is made — nobody gets a link until the draw works.

Pairs are the one edge to watch

With three people, one match gives away the rest. With four people and pairs marked, one match can give away another. Four or more, with no tight pairs, keeps the secret stronger.

The honest part. The organizer holds every link. The organizer could open one. Links are hidden, not locked. The seal is trust, not a lock. Send each link to its person only.

Can someone draw their own name here?

No. The draw here cannot give anyone their own name. If marked pairs make a draw impossible, the site says so before any link is made.

What happens when we draw again?

A new draw makes new links. Links from the old draw still work, and they show the old matches. Send only the new set.
